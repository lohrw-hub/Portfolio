Engineering context

From imaging to a buildable geometry.

Rat anatomy imposes tighter geometric and sensor-placement constraints. The dissertation uses image-derived geometry with conductive composites and embedded probes to support direct measurement and comparison with electromagnetic simulations.

Workflow

MRI / CT → segmentation → mesh cleanup → CAD → molds, tooling and sensor interfaces.
