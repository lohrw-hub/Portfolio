Anatomy as an engineering constraint

In neurotechnology, the anatomy is part of the design. Surface geometry can shape enclosure clearance, instrumentation access, registration, placement fixtures, and how sensors interface with a physical model.

This initial entry focuses on visual access to the supplied surface. Later project material could show the segmentation and CAD workflow, fabricated assemblies, and measurement hardware integrated with the anatomy.

What the viewer reveals

  • The uploaded anatomical surface is represented by 55,090 individual triangles.
  • Orbit and zoom controls allow inspection from different directions.
  • A wireframe mode exposes mesh density and geometric detail.
  • The original STL is available for engineering inspection and further modeling.
