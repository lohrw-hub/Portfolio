Engineering context

From imaging to a buildable geometry.

The macaque platform extends the modeling pipeline beyond human anatomy, using species-specific segmentation and physical mold geometry to preserve relevant regions. Reusable tooling supports consistent fabrication.

MRI / CT → segmentation → mesh cleanup → CAD → molds, tooling and sensor interfaces.
