VCU biomagnetics laboratory

Signal integrity by design.

The differential measurement architecture combined instrumentation amplification with galvanic isolation and isolated power. Early multichannel work revealed 60 Hz noise, connector ground instability and lead-loop pickup. I systematically isolated these causes, revised the grounding arrangement and shortened/re-routed conductors to obtain consistent measurements.

Experiments integrated transient capture through National Instruments acquisition hardware, triggered TMS pulses, robotic coil placement and MATLAB signal processing.

NIH · NHLBI

Femtocurrent measurements.

In earlier X-ray materials research, I helped develop a measurement chamber for femtoamp-scale signals using a Keithley electrometer, oscilloscopes, Faraday shielding and carefully controlled contact and grounding paths. At that scale, fixture materials and mechanical contacts become part of the measurement circuit.
