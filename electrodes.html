Probe development / neural interfaces

Electrodes that belong in the experiment.

Miniature electrodes must preserve their geometry and positioning while limiting distortion of the fields they measure. The design work extends from conductor selection to mechanical anchoring, leads and mating interfaces.

Design constraints

A 3 mm geometry.

Triaxial dipole probes use three orthogonal sensing axes with 3 mm electrode separation. A custom 3D-printed winding tool, twisted leads and molded anchors helped balance manageable assembly with reduced pickup and spatial disturbance.

Related work includes DBS-inspired measurement electrode designs and custom multi-pin connections. These additional connector variants are described from the development record; dedicated photographs/CAD views can be added when available.
